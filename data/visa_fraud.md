# Visa Fraud / Unauthorized Charges

If you suspect fraud or an unauthorized transaction, treat this as a sensitive, high-risk issue.

Recommended actions:
- Do not share full card numbers, CVV, or one-time passwords.
- Immediately contact your bank or card issuer to report unauthorized activity.
- If an account may be compromised, change passwords and enable multi-factor authentication.

Escalation guidance:
- Any mention of fraud, unauthorized activity, hacked accounts, or stolen credentials should be escalated to a human support agent.
